As an educator, I strive to make courses accessible to all students regardless of immigration status. If your status presents obstacles to engaging in specific activities or fulfilling specific criteria, you may request confidential accommodations. You may consult with the [Office of Equity and Compliance](https://www.csulb.edu/equity-compliance) or the [Dream Success Center](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/dream-success-center) for examples of possible accommodations. Such arrangements will not jeopardize your student status, your financial aid, or any other part of your residence. Please advise me if and when you feel comfortable during the semester so that I may make appropriate alterations as needed.
