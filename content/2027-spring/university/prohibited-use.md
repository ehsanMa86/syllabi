---
title: Prohibited Use of Course Content
order: 720
source: CHHS template F2026
---
Students are not permitted to post on the Internet or social media, or transmit, share, or otherwise distribute course content, including recorded and live lectures and discussions, content presented in the chat, PowerPoint presentations, quizzes, tests, exams, and any items posted on Canvas. Course content is for the student's personal academic use only. If students wish to use any of the content outside of class, prior permission from the Professor and all those who are featured in the content (e.g., each student's comment in the chat) must be obtained in writing. Any student who engages in prohibited or unauthorized use of course content will be reported to the University and will be subject to disciplinary action, including but not limited to a course grade of F. A violation of this policy may expose a student to legal liability, both civil and criminal.
