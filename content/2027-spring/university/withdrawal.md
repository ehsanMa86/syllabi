Class withdrawals during the final three weeks of instruction are not permitted except for a very serious and compelling reason such as accident or serious injury that is clearly beyond the student's control and the assignment of an Incomplete grade is not practical (see [Understanding Grades and Grading](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/understanding-grades-and-grading)). Application for withdrawal from CSULB or from a class must be officially filed by the student with Enrollment Services whether the student has ever attended the class or not; otherwise, the student will receive a grade of "WU" (unauthorized withdrawal) in the course. See the CSULB guidelines on [Dropping and Withdrawing](https://www.csulb.edu/student-records/dropping-and-withdrawing) and PS 22-01, Policy on Withdrawals, for more detailed information.
