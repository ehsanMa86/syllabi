Recording class sessions or taking pictures of anyone (e.g., the professor, students, guest lecturers) in the class is not permitted. You do not have permission to post to the Internet or social media, or transmit, share, or otherwise distribute pictures or videos of the instructor and students, course content, including recorded and live lectures and discussions, content presented in the chat, PowerPoint presentations, quizzes, tests, exams, and any items posted on Canvas. Course content is for your personal academic use only. Anyone who engages in prohibited or unauthorized use of course content will be reported to the University and will be subject to disciplinary action, including but not limited to a course grade of F. A violation of this policy may expose you to legal liability, both civil and criminal. If you wish to record, share or disseminate any of the content from our class, prior permission from the instructor and all those who are featured in the content (e.g., each student's comment in the chat) must be obtained in writing.
