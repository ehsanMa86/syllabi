Cheating, plagiarism, and any other violation of academic integrity are not aligned with professional conduct. Work submitted is expected to be original unless your source material is documented using proper citations. Using the ideas or words of another person, even a peer or a web-based resource, as if they were your own, constitutes plagiarism. Use of output generated from artificial intelligence (AI) tools such as ChatGPT as your own, even if you adapt it, is also a violation of academic integrity policies. AI may only be used with instructor permission in accordance with specific assignment guidelines for this course, and must be cited appropriately if used. It is your responsibility to review the University policy on [Academic Integrity](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-senate/policy-academic-integrity-regarding-cheating-and-plagiarism) that governs your participation in courses at CSULB. Additionally, graduate students should consult the [College of Education Graduate Programs Policy on Academic Integrity](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-education/graduate-studies-office/resources-and-policies).
