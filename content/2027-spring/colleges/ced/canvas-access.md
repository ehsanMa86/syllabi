To access this course on Canvas you will need access to the Internet and a supported web browser (Safari, Google Chrome or Firefox). Log in to Canvas with your CSULB Campus ID and BeachID password. If you need technical assistance during the course or would like to report a technical issue with Canvas, Zoom, etc., please contact the [Technology Help Desk](https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1993/Portal/home/). The university provides a variety of [technology resources](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-technology-services/academic-technology-resources-for-students) to students.
