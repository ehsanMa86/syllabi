### Student Success and Advising Center (SSAC)

Students pursuing a degree or credential associated with the College of Education may visit the [SSAC](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-education/college-of-education-student-success-advising-center-ced-ssac). SSAC is dedicated to empowering College of Education students to discover their potential, navigate their academic journey with confidence, and achieve their personal and professional aspirations. We are committed to creating a welcoming environment to serve our diverse students with authentic care and inclusive, equitable practices. Through proactive, reflective, and student-centered strategies, we support success from initial contact through program completion and beyond.

The SSAC location in EED-18 also hosts a variety of campus services throughout the fall and spring semesters to support students, such as staff from Financial Aid, Beach Wellness, and academic support. Visit EED-18, call (562) 985-1105 or email [CED-SSAC@csulb.edu](mailto:CED-SSAC@csulb.edu) to connect.
