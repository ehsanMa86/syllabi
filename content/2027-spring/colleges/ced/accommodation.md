If you have a special need (e.g., disability, long term or acute health concern, etc.) and require reasonable academic accommodations, you are strongly encouraged to register with the Bob Murphy Access Center (BMAC) each semester. Be sure to submit supporting disability documentation to BMAC and apprise faculty of any BMAC verification of accommodations as early in the semester as possible. BMAC is located in the Student Success Center, Room 110 and may also be reached by phone at (562) 985-5401 or via email at [bmac@csulb.edu](mailto:bmac@csulb.edu).
