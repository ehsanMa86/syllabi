To access this course on Canvas you will need access to the Internet and a supported web browser (please note: the preferred web browser for this course is Google Chrome, which minimizes technical issues and responds well to the technology used in this course). Log in through the CSULB Single Sign-On page at [https://csulb.okta.com/](https://csulb.okta.com/) with your CSULB campus ID and password. Once logged in, you will see the course listed on your Canvas dashboard. Click on the title to access the course page.
