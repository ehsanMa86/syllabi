For questions related to your program of study, schedule planning and tutoring, please contact the [COB Center for Student Success](https://www.csulb.edu/college-of-business/center-for-student-success).

Any student who is facing academic or personal challenges due to difficulty in affording groceries/food and/or lacking a safe and stable living environment is urged to contact the [CSULB Student Emergency Intervention & Wellness Program](https://www.csulb.edu/financial-aid-and-scholarships/campus-resources). Additional resources are available via the [Basic Needs Program](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/basic-needs-0). Students can also email [supportingstudents@csulb.edu](mailto:supportingstudents@csulb.edu), call (562) 985-2038, or if comfortable, reach out to the instructor as they may be able to identify additional resources. For mental health assistance, please check out [CSULB Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)](https://www.csulb.edu/student-affairs/counseling-and-psychological-services).
