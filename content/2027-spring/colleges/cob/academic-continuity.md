In the event that the university announces a closure or other emergency that disrupts normal campus operations, I will communicate with you via your CSULB email and/or Canvas with further instructions. Course activities may be modified, moved online, or rescheduled as appropriate. Please ensure early in the semester that you can log in to Canvas (or other designated platform) so you are prepared in case of disruption.
