There is zero tolerance for cheating, plagiarism, or any other violation of academic integrity in this course. Work submitted is assumed to be original unless your source material is documented appropriately using proper citations. Using the ideas or words of another person, even a peer, or a web site, as if it were your own, is plagiarism. Any individual or group caught cheating on homework, or any exam/quiz will be subjected to the full extent of academic actions allowed under University regulations. It is your responsibility to review the University policy on [Cheating and Plagiarism](https://www.csulb.edu/academic-senate/policy-academic-integrity-regarding-cheating-and-plagiarism) that governs your participation in courses at CSULB.
